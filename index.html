Jump rope starter tips

  • Sizing: stand on the middle of the rope — handles should reach your armpits.
  • Small hops, 1–2 inches off the ground, on the balls of your feet.
  • Turn the rope with your wrists, elbows close to your sides.
  • Tripping is normal. Just reset and keep going — or jump without the rope until the timer ends.
